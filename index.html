A lightweight 28-litre day pack with a padded laptop sleeve, breathable back panel and water-resistant fabric. Fits a 15" laptop and a 2 L hydration bladder.

Vinylon F is a durable fabric used primarily in Fjallraven Kanken backpacks. It is made by combining polyvinyl alcohol fibers, which preserves the properties of natural materials and eliminates the need for additional protective coatings. The fibers of Vinylon F swell when exposed to moisture, which makes backpacks made from this material partially waterproof. The fabric can also be cleaned with warm water, a sponge, and mild soap. It has been used in Kanken backpacks since the late 1970s.
